One partner network, from the first missed payment to a real recovery.

When you send a struggling client into this network, they're supported at every stage — before filing, through discharge, and long after. Here's the whole path, and who handles each step.

The starting point

A client is drowning in debt.

They come to you overwhelmed — missed payments, collections, maybe facing foreclosure. Bankruptcy may or may not be the right answer yet.

DebtHelper · before filing

Pre-filing credit counseling.

A court-approved, nonprofit counselor reviews the client's situation and issues the certificate required to file — at no cost to the client.

Should they even file?

If bankruptcy isn't right, DebtHelper offers a debt-management alternative — so you're never the one saying "no," and the client still has a path forward.
