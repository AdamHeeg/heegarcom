Rebuild credit after bankruptcy — the right way.

A bankruptcy can sit on a credit report for years — up to 10 for Chapter 7, 7 for Chapter 13. The score doesn't have to wait that long to climb. A few simple steps, certified nonprofit counselors, and no traps — that's Course 3.

The rebuild path

Six simple steps. Not a maze.

Each step is one idea and one thing to do — with a certified counselor alongside for anyone who wants one.

1

See where you stand

Know the numbers before touching anything.

Do this: pull all three reports (free) — we translate them.
2

Dodge the traps

The fastest way back under is a "fresh start" offer.

Do this: say no to high-fee cards and "erase your bankruptcy" pitches.
3

Open one right account

You rebuild with credit, used lightly.

Do this: open a single secured or builder card and keep it small.
4

Pay on time, always

Payment history is the biggest lever there is.

Do this: set autopay so a payment is never late.
5

Keep balances low

Owing a little beats owing a lot.

Do this: stay well under the limit every month.
6

Give it months, with a counselor

Steady beats fast.

Do this: check in with a counselor and stay the course.
Avoid these

Chains dressed up as fresh starts.

After discharge the offers come fast. A few of them are traps. Know them on sight.

High-fee cards

"Guaranteed approval" cards stacked with fees eat the rebuild before it starts.

Red flag

Subprime auto loans

Sky-high interest on a car you're "lucky to get." You tread water; they win.

Red flag

"We'll erase your bankruptcy"

Impossible and always false. An accurate bankruptcy stays on the report for years.

Scam

Upfront-fee "repair"

Charging before any work is done is a red flag — and often illegal.

Straight answers

Six honest answers.

Can credit really be rebuilt after bankruptcy?

Yes. It's a reset, not a dead end. The right accounts and habits move the number.

How long to reach a strong score?

A score can start moving within months. The bankruptcy reports for up to 7–10 years, but the score doesn't have to wait. No guarantees — steady steps move it.

What does it cost?

The analysis is free, counseling is nonprofit, and setup fees are often waived. No hidden upsell.

Can the bankruptcy be erased?

No. Anyone who promises that is running a scam.

Is this done alone?

No — certified counselors and a whole network, not a video and a shrug.
