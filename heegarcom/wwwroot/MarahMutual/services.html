Credit Repair & Rebuilding

Credit score improvement — including after bankruptcy

Your credit score quietly decides a lot: whether a loan is approved, the interest rate you pay, sometimes even your insurance premium or a hiring decision. After bankruptcy, collections, or a stretch of missed payments, that score can feel permanently stuck — but it is not.

Marah Mutual reviews your reports across all three bureaus, pinpoints what is holding the number down, and builds a realistic, step-by-step plan to strengthen it. We focus on legitimate, lasting improvement — not gimmicks or promises no one can keep.

  • Three-bureau report review (Experian, Equifax, TransUnion)
  • Clear read on what is dragging your score down
  • A personalized rebuilding plan you can actually follow
  • Guidance on utilization, payment history, and healthy new credit
Start with a free analysis

Can I rebuild my credit after bankruptcy?

Yes. A discharge or past collections do not fix your credit on their own, but they also do not make recovery impossible. We build a step-by-step plan to strengthen your profile over time.

How long does credit improvement take?

It varies by situation, and no one can honestly guarantee a specific number by a specific date. We focus on consistent, legitimate steps that move your profile in the right direction.

Pay Less For Coverage

Insurance rate optimization

Many people do not realize that in a lot of states, insurers use a credit-based insurance score to help set premiums. That means the same profile work that lifts your credit can also, over time, lower what you pay for coverage.

We help you understand the connection and strengthen the factors that quietly drive your rates.

  • Understand how your profile affects premiums
  • Strengthen the factors insurers weigh
  • Position for better rates as your profile improves
See where you stand

Can improving my credit lower my insurance rates?

In many states insurers use a credit-based insurance score, so a stronger profile can translate into better premiums over time.

Which types of insurance does this affect?

It most commonly comes up with auto and home policies, depending on your state and insurer.
