The path

Six simple steps. Not a maze.

Here's the path to stay unconquered — and a coach in your corner if you want one. Each step is one idea and one thing to do.

1

See where you stand

Know your numbers before you touch anything.
Your move: pull your three reports (free) — we translate them.

2

Dodge the traps

The fastest way back under is a "fresh start" offer.
Your move: say no to high-fee cards and "erase your bankruptcy" pitches.

3

Open one right account

You rebuild with credit, used lightly.
Your move: open a single secured or builder card and keep it small.

4

Pay on time, always

Payment history is the biggest lever there is.
Your move: set autopay so you're never late.

5

Keep balances low

Owing a little beats owing a lot.
Your move: stay well under your limit every month.

6

Give it months, with a coach

Steady beats fast.
Your move: check in with your coach and stay the course.

Avoid these

Chains dressed up as fresh starts.

After discharge the offers come fast. A few of them are traps. Know them on sight.

×

High-fee cards

"Guaranteed approval" cards stacked with fees eat your rebuild before it starts.

×

Subprime auto loans

Sky-high interest on a car you're "lucky to get." You tread water; they win.

×

"We'll erase your bankruptcy"

Impossible and always false. An accurate bankruptcy stays for years.

×

Upfront-fee "repair"

Charging before any work is done is a red flag — and often illegal.
